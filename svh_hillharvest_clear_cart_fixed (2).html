Our story

A family business rooted in Kodaikanal

Welcome to SVH HillHarvest, a family-run business based in the hills near Kodaikanal, Tamil Nadu.

We bring together a selection of hill-grown produce and food products, including avocados, hill garlic, honey, chocolates and seasonal fruits and vegetables. Some of our produce comes from our own family growing activities, while other products are sourced from local producers.

We aim to provide clear product information, careful packing and personal service. Contact us to check availability, confirm prices and discuss delivery options across Tamil Nadu.
